How to connect

  1. Generate or enter a room code.
  2. Share it with people you trust.
  3. Join from each browser/device.
  4. Sources press Start transmission.
  5. Listeners press play on the audio tiles.

A room code is not a secret. Use a PIN and share both details privately.
